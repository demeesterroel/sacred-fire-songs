# Initial Concept
Sacred Fire Songs is a collaborative web application for ceremonial musicians (Guitareros) and ceremony participants. Its mission is to bridge oral tradition and accessibility by providing a digital songbook with accurate lyrics, chords, melodies (ABC notation), and real-time transposition, all optimized for offline use in remote environments.

# Product Definition: Sacred Fire Songs

## Vision
To be the definitive digital guardian of oral ceremonial traditions, bridging ancient wisdom with modern accessibility. Sacred Fire Songs is more than a tool; it is a digital companion for the fire, ensuring that medicine songs are preserved, learned, and shared with absolute accuracy and reverence.

## Target Audience
- **Admins & Curators (Primary Focus):** The keepers of the digital flame. They require robust tools to import, verify, and organize the song library with surgical precision.
- **Guitareros (Musicians):** Ceremonial leaders who rely on real-time transposition, accurate ChordPro formatting, and melodic references to guide the circle.
- **Participants:** Community members who seek deep engagement through large, readable lyrics and accurate translations.

## Core Goals
1. **Pristine Curation:** Provide curators with elite tools (Smart Import, ChordPro editing) to ensure the Nina Urku collection and future songs are perfectly documented.
2. **Ceremonial Immersion:** Maintain a "Mystical/Ceremonial" aesthetic—grayscale, glassmorphism, and minimal distractions—to respect the sacred space of the ceremony.
3. **Resilient Access:** Ensure the platform remains fully functional in remote, offline environments (PWA) where the fire is most often found.
4. **Accurate Learning:** Bridge the gap between hearing a song and leading it through high-quality metadata, ABC notation for melodies, and synchronized audio.

## Key Features
- **Smart Song Management:** Drag-and-drop ChordPro/Text imports with automatic metadata detection for rapid, high-quality curation.
- **Dynamic Musical Toolkit:** Real-time transposition, Capo suggestions, and Tuning references tailored for the Guitarero.
- **Spiritual UI:** A "Mystical" design system using emerald/amber accents, flame micro-interactions, and dark-mode glassmorphism.
- **Offline First:** Full PWA support with React Query caching for reliable use in nature.
- **Role-Based Governance:** Strict RLS policies and admin controls to protect the integrity of the songbook.

## Success Metrics
- **Curator Efficiency:** Time taken to move a song from "Raw Paste" to "Verified Public."
- **Collection Depth:** Completion and verification of the Nina Urku library.
- **Reliability:** Zero-failure performance during offline mobile use in ceremonial contexts.
